### AS5600 4-wire (your cable, inserted from "left side")
┌───────────────┬────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┐
│ Wire (color)  │ AS5600 signal      │ Arduino UNO/Nano connection  │
├───────────────┼────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────┤
│ Yellow        │ SCL                │ A5  (SCL)                    │
│ Blue          │ SDA                │ A4  (SDA)                    │
│ Black         │ GND                │ GND                          │
│ Red           │ VCC                │ 3.3 V  (or 5 V per module)   │
└───────────────┴────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────┘
Notes:
- UNO/Nano: SDA=A4, SCL=A5. (On many boards there are also dedicated SDA/SCL pins.) :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}
- AS5600 modules typically accept **3.3 V or 5 V** (check your board’s silk/manual). :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}
- Keep I²C wires short and twisted if possible. Most breakout boards already include pull‑ups.


### AS5600 4-wire and servo
External 5V PSU (+) ───────► Servo RED (V+)
External 5V PSU (GND) ──┬──► Servo BROWN/BLACK (GND)
                         ├──► Arduino GND
                         └──► AS5600 GND

Arduino D9  ───────────────► Servo ORANGE/YELLOW (Signal)
Arduino 3.3V or 5V ───────► AS5600 VCC  (match your module spec)
Arduino A4  ───────────────► AS5600 SDA
Arduino A5  ───────────────► AS5600 SCL

* Do NOT power the servo from the Arduino 5V pin if the servo draws real current.
* Keep all grounds tied together (servo PSU GND ↔ Arduino GND ↔ AS5600 GND).
