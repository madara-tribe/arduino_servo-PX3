Arduino Nano ESP32 (ESP32-S3)            Bus Servo Driver (Akizuki 131227)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
GND   --------------------------------->  GND
RX0 (D0, RX_PIN=0) --------------------->  RX
TX1 (D1, TX_PIN=1) --------------------->  TX

Bus Servo Driver  <--------------------->  ST3215-HS
(3-pin servo port “D V G”)                 (3-wire bus servo cable)
D (DATA) --------------------------------> DATA
V (V+)   --------------------------------> V+
G (GND)  --------------------------------> GND
